# Asendus

Helps one school's substitution coordinator cover absent teachers' lessons and break duties, day by day.

## Language

**Absence**:
One teacher missing on one date, for a range of periods.
_Avoid_: Leave, sick day

**Missed lesson**:
One lesson of a teacher that falls inside their absence. How much a teacher was away is measured in missed lessons, not days, so a one-lesson absence weighs 1.
_Avoid_: Absent day, absence count

**Substitution row**:
One lesson or break duty of an absent teacher on that date, with who covers it.
_Avoid_: Asendus row, slot

**Substitute**:
A teacher picked to cover a substitution row; a row can have several.
_Avoid_: Replacement, cover teacher

**Cover**:
One substitution row a given teacher is a substitute on. Shared rows give each substitute a full cover; a row with only free text is nobody's cover. A **lesson cover** and a **duty cover** are counted apart, since a break duty is far less work.
_Avoid_: Assignment, shift
